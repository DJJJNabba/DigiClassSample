Generate Learning Content

Use AI to draft practice quizzes or flashcard sets on any topic. Drafts are saved as pending until a teacher approves them for the shared library.

How it works

  1. 1 Choose a subject and enter a topic you want to study
  2. 2 AI drafts a structured set of questions or flashcards
  3. 3 Your draft is saved privately as pending
  4. 4 A teacher reviews it and approves it
  5. 5 Approved content joins the shared library for everyone
💡 Tip Specific topics produce sharper questions. Try “Photosynthesis light reactions” rather than just “Biology”.
